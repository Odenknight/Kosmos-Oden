# Search and sync limits in 0.9.6

E-Z ranks passages using words in the question and in the vault's Markdown notes. Try a distinctive name or phrase if your first question misses. Open the source to judge a weak match. The status shows when indexing is underway and how many oversized or unreadable files were skipped. Attachments and text in images are outside this search.

The local view covers the user's vault. Desktop assistants have a separate read policy and can receive content from the folders you share. The optional semantic search for assistant questions has its own model and index settings.

Nextcloud WebDAV is the built-in sync provider. S3-compatible storage is planned. Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive and paid managed services are roadmap ideas. [Roadmap](https://github.com/Odenknight/Kosmos-Oden/blob/0.9.6/ROADMAP.md)
